Core Methodology

Six-Step Risk Assessment Cycle

Continuous cybersecurity posture management simplified for small & medium organizations.

01

Identify

Inventory critical business assets, sensitive customer data, vulnerabilities, and relevant threat actors.

02

Assess

Estimate threat likelihood (1-5) and potential operational or financial business impact (1-5).

03

Score Risk

Calculate risk magnitude using the core formula: Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact.

04

Mitigate

Prioritize high-scoring risks and deploy cost-effective technical and administrative controls.

05

Respond

Establish emergency response protocols to detect, contain, and quickly recover from incidents.

06

Review

Periodically re-evaluate security posture, audit control effectiveness, and adapt to new cyber threats.
